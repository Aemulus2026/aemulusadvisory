Aemula
Latin · aemulari · "to close the gap"

The Romans used aemulari to describe the discipline of striving to close the gap between where you are and where you intended to be. Not rivalry. Not competition. The act of making reality match intention.

In manufacturing, that gap has a cost. It is the distance between what your operation should be producing and what it actually produces. Between the margin your business case promised and the margin your P&L shows. Between the efficiency your new system was supposed to deliver and the reality six months after go-live.

Aemula finds that gap, puts a number on it, and builds the system that closes it permanently.
