What information we collect

  • Contact details you give us when you email, call or message us: your name, job title, organisation, email address and phone number.
  • The content of your enquiry and any later correspondence.
  • Engagement information if you or your organisation become a client: contract, billing and project details, and the business information needed to deliver the work. We ask clients not to share personal data in project materials unless it is needed.
  • Technical information such as your IP address and browser type, which our website host and font provider receive automatically when you visit.

We do not knowingly collect special category data, such as health information, and we do not collect information from children.

Cookies

Our website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads. Google's own privacy policy applies to that.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the business, under contracts that require them to protect it. These include our email and IT providers, our website host, our accountant, and our bank. We may also disclose information where the law requires it. We never sell personal information.

International transfers

Some of our providers store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or approved safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, to protect your information.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: up to 2 years from our last contact.
  • Client and financial records: 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as required for tax purposes.
  • Mailing preferences: until you ask us to stop. We keep a record of opt-outs so we do not contact you again.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your information
  • ask us to restrict or stop using your information, including objecting to processing based on legitimate interests
  • ask for your information in a portable format

To use any of these rights, email bamidele.thomas@aemulusadvisory.com. We will respond within one month and there is normally no charge.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice
